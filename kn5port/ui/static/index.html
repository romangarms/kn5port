Getting your Forza Horizon files

kn5port converts Forza Horizon (2012, Xbox 360) from a copy you own. It comes with no game files and doesn't get around any copy protection. Pick any one of these:

A Games on Demand (GOD) package

The way the game is stored on an Xbox 360 hard drive or USB stick, for the digital version or a disc installed to the drive. Copy the whole title folder off the drive:

  • Content\0000000000000000\4D5309C9  (4D5309C9 is Forza Horizon's title ID)

It holds 00007000\<long name> and a <long name>.data folder of Data0000, Data0001… files. Pick the 4D5309C9 folder, or any folder inside it.

A disc image (.iso)

An image of your own game disc, made with a disc-dumping tool and a compatible drive. Pick the .iso file. Images of the full disc and trimmed images both work.

Extracted files

A folder holding default.xex and media\, for example the folder you run in the Xenia emulator, or the output of extract-xiso.

For a GOD package or an ISO, kn5port reads it directly and copies out only what the converter uses (about 4 GB) into its work folder. You don't need to convert anything yourself.

If kn5port can't read your copy

Turn it into extracted files with other tools, then pick that folder: a GOD package with god2iso, then the ISO with extract-xiso -x.

Never share converted levels or game files. The result is for you to drive on this computer.

Before you convert anything

kn5port converts tracks and maps you already have into BeamNG levels on this computer. The result is a derivative of someone else's work, so:

• Never share converted levels — not as mods, uploads, or in bug reports.
• Check each track's license. Some forbid conversions to other games, even for personal use.
• Protected / encrypted tracks are skipped; kn5port doesn't get around protection.
• Forza Horizon is converted only from your own copy of the game. kn5port includes no game files.
