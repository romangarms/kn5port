Before you convert anything

kn5port converts tracks you already have into BeamNG levels on this computer. The result is a derivative of someone else's work, so:

• Never share converted levels — not as mods, uploads, or in bug reports.
• Check each track's license. Some forbid conversions to other games, even for personal use.
• Protected / encrypted tracks are skipped; kn5port doesn't get around protection.
